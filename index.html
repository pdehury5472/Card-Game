ଦଶ ଗଣ୍ଡିଆ - Gameplay & Beranga Rules

1. Card Hierarchy: Heart 2 (👑 1st Power) > Spade 2 (⚔️ 2nd Power) > Trump Ace (✨ 3rd Power) > Trump Cards > High Suit Cards.

2. Beranga Rule (ବେରଙ୍ଗ): If no Trump suit is set, Heart 2 and Spade 2 remain the highest cards in game. All other suits follow standard ranking (Ace down to 2). If void in led suit, any card can be played.

3. Trick Cutting: Playing a Colour card on a void non-colour trick captures all 4 cards and Gandas. Over-trumping with a higher Colour card takes the trick.

4. Swipe Control: Pull/drag cards upward to throw them into the trick area.
