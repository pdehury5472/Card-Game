Dasha Gandia Rules

1. Trump (ରଙ୍ଗ): Made after receiving 9 cards. Options include 4 suits or ବେରଙ୍ଗ (No Trump).

2. Rotating Trump Maker: Trump selection moves sequentially between players (A ➔ B ➔ C ➔ D) every round.

3. Skip Trump: Automatically sets Trump from 12th card suit. If 12th card is ❤️ 2 or ♠️ 2, it becomes ବେରଙ୍ଗ.

4. Special Powers: Heart 2 (1st) > Spade 2 (2nd) > Trump Ace (3rd).

5. Drop Restrictions: Heart 2 & Spade 2 cannot be played on 1st or 13th ଗଣ୍ଡା, and cannot open any round.
