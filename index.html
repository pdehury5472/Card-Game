📖 Dasa Gandia Rules

52-card deck: 4 suits × 13 ranks.
♥2 is Joker #1 and highest card. ♠2 is Joker #2 and second-highest.
10 key Ganda cards: 4 Aces + 4 Kings + ♥2 + ♠2.
Bids support 0–13. Bid 10, 11, 12 or 13 uses a 2× multiplier.
Four seats: A+C vs B+D. Anti-clockwise turn order is supported by the room state.
Colour can be Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs or Beranga.
13 tricks per round. Target can be 50 or 100.
Joker-in-Trick-13 penalty of −25 is preserved as a scoring rule.
Free Card Drop: this web version intentionally removes card-play restrictions. You may play any card at any time. No forced follow-suit, no forced over-trump, no Joker lead restriction, no Trick-1 restriction and no Trick-13 card-play restriction.
